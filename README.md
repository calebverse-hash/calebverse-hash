### Research snapshot

From one preliminary experimental comparison of a classical mixture density network (MDN) and a hybrid parameterized-quantum-circuit MDN (PQC–MDN):

- **Validation negative log-likelihood:** classical **−0.3874** vs. hybrid **−0.5258** (lower is better for this metric).
- **Mean analytic overlap area across 30 distances:** classical **0.7305** vs. hybrid **0.7805**.
- **PyTorch–Qiskit feature comparison:** maximum absolute difference of approximately **2.98 × 10⁻⁷** for the tested inputs.

*These are outputs from a particular research experiment, not a claim that I independently developed the models or that the hybrid method is universally superior. See the [research case study](projects/quantum-research.md).*
