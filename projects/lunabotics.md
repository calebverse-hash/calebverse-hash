# NASA Lunabotics — Rover Electrical & Computer Systems

**Role:** Electrical / computer lead, student team  
**Stage:** In progress

## Goal
Develop reliable, testable electrical systems for a student Lunabotics rover in coordination with the mechanical and software team.

## Current scope
- Turn mission requirements into subsystem and interface requirements.
- Plan batteries, power distribution/protection, sensing, motors, motor drives, and compute/control.
- Estimate energy demand and prepare measurable power budgets.
- Define wiring, integration order, interfaces and subsystem verification procedures.
- Evaluate components for practicality, cost and serviceability.

## Next artifacts
Electrical block diagram, power budget, bill of materials, wiring diagram, photos, prototype test results and released firmware links.

**Status:** Planning and system engineering work are in progress. No final design, measured performance, or competition outcome is claimed here.
